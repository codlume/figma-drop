Check 5b (Reveal in Finder) failed in one full proof run. This records the trace that settled it.
Build: the failing commit, real Electron, real preload, library, and receiver, scratch library.
Scripts stopped after 5b, so no Trash call ran.

Cause 1: the proof pressed Reveal while Copy path was still running.
Check 5a waited for the clipboard, which main writes before it replies.
Instrumented proof flow, 8 runs, 6 failed:
  5 runs pressed Reveal 16-18 ms after Copy path, with the button locked. No click event fired, and 5b failed.
    In each of them Copy path's reply landed 16-17 ms after the press.
  3 runs pressed 33-34 ms after Copy path, after its reply, so the click fired. 2 passed. 1 failed from cause 2.
With one added line, await session.waitText('Path copied'), on the same build:
  6 of 6 runs passed.
Verdict: a proof race. The one-action-at-a-time lock did what it is meant to. Fixed in check 5a.

Cause 2: the proof required Finder to take OS focus.
Run 7 showed "Shown in Finder", so the app finished the action. The window then kept focus for 15 s.
Focused trace, frontmost app read with lsappinfo around each real Reveal:
  14 of 14 moved the front app from this app to Finder.
Across every run that reached that step: 22 of 23 handed off. The one miss did not recur.
Cause of that miss: not proven. Other threads run Electron on this Mac at the same time, which may
be related, and this trace does not show that.
Verdict: no renderer defect shown. The app reports success only after main returns from
shell.showItemInFolder. Check 5b now asserts Finder is frontmost, and reports UNVERIFIED rather than
FAIL when another app already held the front before the click.

Final full run (verify-renderer.txt): 5b passed, and 5b-front reported UNVERIFIED because another
process named Electron held the front at that click.
Same build, stopped after 5b, 3 more runs: this app held the front, and the Finder check ran and passed.
